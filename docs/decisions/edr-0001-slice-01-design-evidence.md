# EDR 0001: Slice 01 Design Evidence

**Status:** Approved

**Date:** 2026-08-03

## User problem addressed

The first implementation must be visually and behaviorally directed before coding, while avoiding
weeks of duplicate design production for two low-motion scenes whose primary risks are value
comprehension, provenance, adaptive layout, accessibility, and performance.

## Options considered

1. Require a Figma file and complete seven-scene prototype video before any Flutter product code.
2. Begin Flutter from broad product documents and let implementation discover visual behavior.
3. Authorize only TA-01 and TA-02 from an approved Design Bible, responsive storyboards, and bounded
   implementation packet; retain motion/video evidence before later transition-heavy scenes.

## Chosen direction

Choose option 3.

The Quiet Orbit Design Bible and TA-01/TA-02 storyboards are sufficient design authority for Slice
01. Antigravity implements them without inventing product direction, and Codex reviews screenshots,
interaction behavior, accessibility, and performance across five approved viewports.

A complete flow prototype video is not required for Slice 01. Before TA-03 through TA-07 are
implemented, the team must review a bounded transition study covering private commitment, human
perspective reveal, consequence, reconsideration, Mind Shift, and reduced motion. The study may be
a lightweight interactive prototype or recorded motion reference; it need not duplicate final
Flutter production code.

## Design and pacing rationale

- TA-01 and TA-02 rely primarily on hierarchy, copy, trust labels, role equality, and adaptive
  composition.
- The highest-risk motion concepts begin with independent commitment, perspective arrival, and
  thought-object lineage in later scenes.
- Real implementation screenshots and measured behavior provide better evidence for the first two
  scenes than a separate video approximation.
- This preserves experience-first decision making without turning documentation artifacts into an
  indefinite design phase.

## Evidence

- Approved Mind Design Bible version 1.0.
- Approved seven-scene storyboard pack version 1.0.
- Approved adaptive and performance budgets.
- Slice 01 packet includes exact composition, accessibility, state, test, and review requirements.

## Reconsideration triggers

Revisit this decision if:

- Antigravity must invent a fundamental visual or transition rule to complete TA-01 or TA-02;
- compact and expanded compositions cannot share the approved semantic flow;
- user testing shows the written storyboards fail to communicate intended pacing;
- Slice 01 review exposes repeated visual inconsistency that a reference board would have prevented;
- work on TA-03 begins without the required later-scene transition study.
