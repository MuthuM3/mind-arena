# ADR-0001: Solo Decision Arena

**Status:** Superseded by ADR-0003 and ADR-0004

**Original date:** 2026-07-29

## Original decision

V1 would be a solo five-to-eight-minute Decision Arena in which a participant committed to an idea,
received an AI pressure test, reconsidered, and saw a Thinking Replay.

## Why it was superseded

Further product discovery identified that the differentiated value is not an AI challenging one
person. It is AI creating conditions in which real people expose one another to perspectives they
would not generate alone.

The solo Decision Arena remained too close to the one-person-to-AI pattern and delayed validation of
the core human network.

## Preserved learning

These ideas remain:

- commit before influence;
- make assumptions and reasons visible;
- revision is legitimate;
- reflection completes the experience;
- the interface is structured rather than chat-shaped.

They now operate inside a human-to-human Perspective Sprint.
