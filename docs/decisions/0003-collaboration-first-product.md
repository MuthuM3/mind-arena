# ADR-0003: Collaboration-First Product Thesis

**Status:** Accepted; consumer framing refined by ADR-0005

**Date:** 2026-07-29

## Context

The original concept emphasized AI-generated thinking games and later a solo Decision Arena.
Product exploration revealed a more differentiated role for AI: facilitate interactions in which
humans create insights together.

Entertainment alone is difficult to sustain, while general brainstorming is too broad. The product
needs a narrow repeatable action that proves the collaboration thesis.

## Decision

Mind Arena’s durable value is a facilitated human-perspective network. ADR-0005 later refined the
consumer expression into an interactive social thinking game without changing this human-first
thesis.

Its durable value is:

- real human perspectives;
- structured constructive engagement;
- AI-supported fairness, focus, and reflection;
- visible evolution of individual and shared thinking.

AI never impersonates a participant or determines a winner.

The north-star outcome is Meaningful Perspective Exchange, not messages, model usage, debate wins,
or time spent.

## Consequences

Positive:

- differentiates from AI chat and social media;
- makes human diversity an asset;
- supports consumer, community, and team paths;
- creates a durable role for AI independent of provider.

Costs and risks:

- network cold start becomes central;
- identity, consent, moderation, and trust are required earlier;
- human supply and quality constrain growth;
- safety operations become a core competency;
- offline use cannot deliver the full core experience.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Solo AI thinking coach:** useful but insufficiently distinct from answer assistants.
- **Entertainment game catalog:** broad content burden without a durable human network.
- **General brainstorming platform:** too broad for an initial repeatable habit.
- **Winner-based debate:** creates engagement at the cost of the desired thinking behavior.

## Review trigger

Review only if repeated well-run cohorts cannot identify a distinct benefit from real human
perspective compared with solo AI.
