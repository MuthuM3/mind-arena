## 1. Mind Shift Generation Engine

The Mind Shift engine generates a personal reflection summary capturing how a participant's reasoning evolved during perspective exchange.

### Source-Linked Fields Specification

Every Mind Shift summary MUST contain eight complete source-linked fields without abbreviation:

1. **Initial Choice** (`InitialChoice`): The initial 3 selected roles (full exact role names) and exact original reason text from Stage 2.
2. **Engaged Perspective** (`EngagedPerspective`): The exact provenance tag and exact full text of the specific perspective card selected by the participant as their engagement target in Stage 4.
3. **Response Type** (`ResponseType`): The selected response mode (`Build`, `Challenge`, or `Question`) and the exact full response text from Stage 5.
4. **Consequence Rule** (`ConsequenceRule`): The deterministic consequence rule ID (e.g., `CONS-01-NAV-DEFICIT`) and exact user-facing consequence copy applied in Stage 5.
5. **Final Choice** (`FinalChoice`): The final 3 selected roles (full exact role names) and exact final reason text from Stage 6.
6. **Added Consideration** (`AddedConsideration`): Detailed summary of any new trade-off, functional dependency, or risk factor evaluated during the session.
7. **Convergence / Divergence** (`ConvergenceDivergence`): Classification of the final choice outcome (`Unchanged`, `Refined`, or `Changed`) and explicit description of alignment or divergence.
8. **Unresolved Question** (`UnresolvedQuestion`): Full text of open dilemma or ongoing tension identified by the participant.

### Synthesis Labeling and Participant Verification Rule
Generated Mind Shift summaries MUST be visibly prefixed with `[Arena Engine Synthesis]` and MUST include the explicit verification notice:
`[Arena Engine Synthesis] (Participant Verification: This summary is generated by the Arena Engine. You may accept, reject, or correct any field if it misrepresents your rationale.)`

## 2. Aggregate Data Thresholds and Privacy Protection Rules

To prevent privacy leakage and false social claims, aggregate metrics enforce strict statistical rules:

### Threshold Requirements
- **Choice Distribution Threshold**: Aggregate choice distributions are displayed ONLY when at least **10 eligible completed sessions** exist from the identical arena version (M1.1) and compatible adult pool.
- **Cell Suppression Rule**: Within an eligible distribution table, any cell with fewer than **3 observations** MUST be suppressed (displayed as `< 3` or `N/A`).
- **Consideration Frequency Threshold**: Claims regarding consideration frequency (e.g., "common trade-off" or "rare consideration") require at least **20 eligible completed sessions** AND at least **3 observations** of that specific consideration. Otherwise, neutral insufficient-data copy MUST be displayed (e.g., `"Insufficient cohort data to evaluate consideration frequency."`).
- **Excluded Records**: Editorial cards, test fixtures, demonstration-only sessions, withdrawn contributions, minor pool records, ineligible sessions, and incomplete sessions are strictly excluded from all aggregate calculations.

### Strict Prohibition on Live and Comparative Claims
- No percentile rank comparisons, live social status indicators, live player counts, or real-time consensus metrics are calculated or displayed.
- No popularity rankings or moral correctness percentages are generated.

## 3. Spoiler-Safe Result Card Specification and No-Auto-Share Privacy Rule

The result card is a compact, shareable summary designed for external reflection.

### No-Auto-Share Privacy Rule
- **Private Creation by Default**: Mind Shift summaries and Result Cards are created strictly in private participant view.
- **Explicit Participant Preview**: The participant MUST preview their generated result card in private view before any sharing option is presented.
- **Separate Affirmative Action Required**: Result cards are NEVER automatically shared, published, posted to social feeds, or transmitted to third parties. Sharing occurs ONLY when the participant takes a separate, deliberate affirmative action (e.g., clicking "Export / Copy Result Card").

### Content Constraints
- **Spoiler-Safe**: Excludes scenario plot twists or specific role details that would ruin the initial private choice for future participants.
- **Privacy-Preserving**: Contains NO third-party raw words or quotes from other participants.
- **No Prohibited Scores**: Displays NO intelligence, empathy, greed, trustworthiness, openness, persuasion, personality, morality, protected-trait, or human-worth scores.
- **Provenanced System Copy**: Uses strictly provenanced system text (`[Arena Engine]`).
