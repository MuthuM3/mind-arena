# Task

## Outcome

Describe one user-visible or operational result.

## Product authority

Link the exact approved requirement, roadmap slice, storyboard, or ADR that governs this task.

## Context

Explain why the change is needed and what baseline already exists.

## Requirements

- State each required behavior in observable, testable language.
- Include loading, empty-pool, timeout, offline, and recovery behavior where applicable.
- Include accessibility, consent, moderation, and privacy behavior where applicable.

## Non-goals

- State what must not be built or changed.
- Keep product strategy, safety policy, privacy policy, and unrelated milestones out of scope.

## Acceptance criteria

- List exact pass/fail conditions.
- Name the deterministic checks expected to pass.
- Identify any manual device, visual, safety, or research validation still required.

## Allowed boundary

- List the narrow files or modules expected to change.

## Constraints and risks

- Note compatibility, security, data, migration, performance, rollout, and deadline constraints.
- Identify decisions that require human escalation.
