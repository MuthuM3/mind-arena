## Outcome

A first-time adult can open Mind Arena, understand that Today’s Arena is a short asynchronous
human-perspective experience, enter Mars Rescue, and understand the six roles, public guardian
relationship, immutable facts, and three-seat decision in under 30 seconds.

The slice is local, deterministic, backend-free, live-AI-free, and usable across compact, medium,
and expanded windows.

## In scope

### Domain

Add only the pure Dart objects required by TA-01 and TA-02:

- arena identity and immutable content version;
- title, premise, stakes line, estimated duration, locale, and adult audience classification;
- asynchronous/social truth label data;
- role ID, title, functional description, and optional public relationship;
- immutable scenario facts;
- decision constraint: select exactly three of six roles;
- package availability and validation failure types.

Models are immutable, equality-testable, and independent of Flutter, Riverpod, JSON, and data
implementations. Do not model contribution, matching, AI, final choice, or backend fields yet.

### Repository contract and local data

- Define the smallest domain repository contract that loads the current local arena package.
- Implement one deterministic local Mars Rescue repository under `data/local/`.
- Keep package mapping in the data boundary.
- Validate required IDs, exact role count, unique role IDs, guardian relationship targets, nonempty
  facts, and the three-seat constraint before returning a usable package.
- Support deterministic success, unavailable, and malformed-package test modes through injected
  local configuration—not timers or hidden global flags.

No HTTP client, database, file download, remote configuration, or cloud SDK.

### Experience state

Add the smallest controller/state model for:

- invitation loading;
- invitation ready;
- package unavailable;
- package invalid;
- entering the situation;
- situation ready;
- facts panel open/closed;
- interruption-safe current scene state.

State transitions are explicit and independently testable. Widgets do not call repositories.

### Design foundation

Implement only tokens required by this slice:

- approved semantic colors;
- type roles with system-font fallback;
- spacing, radius, focus, and touch-target tokens;
- compact, medium, and expanded window classes;
- reduced-motion preference access;
- constrained readable content width.

Do not bundle Manrope or Inter until font files and licenses are separately verified. Use the
approved system-font fallback in this slice.

### Components

Create only reusable components exercised by both scenes or clearly required across the near-term
journey:

- arena frame/progress header;
- provenance or trust label;
- primary and secondary action controls;
- situation/fact panel;
- role card in nonselectable TA-02 form;
- loading, unavailable, and invalid-package states;
- visible keyboard-focus treatment.

Avoid a broad component catalog. Scene-specific composition remains in the arena boundary.

### TA-01 Invitation

Render the approved content contract:

- Today’s Arena
- Mars Rescue
- approved stakes line
- approximately three minutes
- asynchronous/not-live truth
- real-human-perspective promise without implying immediate supply
- adult research classification
- Enter arena action

Do not render player count, percentile, streak, countdown, difficulty, model name, subscription, or
fake activity.

### TA-02 Situation

- Reveal the immutable premise and three-seat constraint.
- Present all six role cards with equal weight and stable order.
- Present the Coordinator–Young Dependent guardian relationship clearly.
- Keep Facts and rules reviewable.
- State that there is no approved correct list.
- Provide `I understand the situation` as the terminal action for this slice.
- The action does not navigate into an unimplemented private-choice screen; show a deliberate
  development boundary or end the preview cleanly according to the existing router pattern.

## Adaptive requirements

### Compact

- Verify 390 × 844 and 844 × 390.
- One focused column with staged context.
- Primary action remains reachable without overlaying scaled text.
- Role layout may reflow from two columns to one based on local card width.

### Medium

- Verify 768 × 1024.
- Situation stage may use a supporting facts rail.
- Support touch, pointer, keyboard, portrait, landscape, and split-view pressure.

### Expanded

- Verify 1024 × 768 and 1440 × 900.
- Use deliberate progress, stage, and context regions.
- Constrain reading line length; do not center a fixed phone canvas.

Across all classes, resizing preserves current scene, facts-panel state, focus intent, and scroll
intent. Layout decisions use constraints, not platform names.

## Accessibility requirements

- Logical headings and semantic order.
- Decorative atmosphere excluded from semantics.
- Role card exposes title, function, and public relationship.
- Three-seat constraint appears in accessible scene summary.
- Minimum 48 × 48 target size.
- Visible focus for every control.
- Enter/Space activation and predictable Tab/Shift-Tab order.
- Escape closes dismissible facts overlay without losing scene state.
- 200% system text scale without clipping, overlap, hidden content, or inaccessible action.
- Reduced motion produces equivalent immediate composition and focus behavior.
- Sound, motion, hover, and color are never the only information source.

## Performance requirements

Measure in profile or release mode where the platform permits:

- warm start to usable invitation: at most 1.0 second;
- representative cold start: target at most 2.5 seconds;
- local input visual response: at most 100 milliseconds;
- at least 99% of measured journey frames within the display frame budget;
- no unexpected layout shift after stable content appears;
- no sustained memory growth across ten invitation-to-situation repetitions.

Record device, OS, build mode, viewport, measurement method, result, and any variance. Debug timing
is not acceptance evidence.
