# TA-01 — Invitation

## Purpose

Create quiet curiosity and communicate the truthful product contract (~3 minutes, shared with real people, asynchronous, not live) before account or scenario effort.

## User State Before Entry

First-time or returning participant opening Mind Arena for a daily reflection session.

## Intended Emotion on Entry

Quiet curiosity.

## Intended Emotion on Exit

Intrigue, clarity, and readiness to enter the Arena.

## Core User Question

"What is today's challenge, and what am I committing to?"

## Primary Action

`Enter arena`

## Secondary Actions

- `Review facts sheet`
- `Leave arena`

## Information Hierarchy

1. Arena Title ("Today’s Arena")
2. Dilemma Title & Stakes Line ("Mars Rescue — One shuttle. Three seats. Six lives and six responsibilities.")
3. Duration & Social Truth ("~3 minutes · Shared with real people · Not live")
4. Audience & Eligibility Label ("Adult research pool")
5. Primary Action ("Enter arena")

## Eye Flow

Heading → Premise → Social Truth / Duration badges → Primary Action.

## Content

Deterministic approved copy for Mars Rescue premise. No countdown timers, player counts, streaks, difficulty ratings, or model brand names.

## AI Presence

Deterministic approved copy only. No local or cloud model task.

## Human Perspective Presence

States social truth ("Shared with real people · Not live"). No active perspectives displayed in TA-01.

## Interaction Sequence

1. View title, premise, duration (~3 minutes), and social truth.
2. Verify adult eligibility disclosure.
3. Select `Enter arena`.

## Scene States

### Initial
Available local package loaded.

### Active
Premise stage presented with interactive `Enter arena` action.

### Loading
Package loading state.

### Success
Transition triggered to TA-02 — Situation.

### Error
Package unavailable or expired error message displayed.

### Offline
Supported with pre-cached local package.

### Restored
Resume previous session state.

## Motion Intent

Motion explains entry and atmosphere without delay or forced animations. Control feedback 100–160 ms; scene transition 280–420 ms.

- Focus begins on scene heading, not primary action.
- Decorative orbital graphics hidden from screen readers.
- 200% text scaling and reduced motion supported.

## Reduced Motion
