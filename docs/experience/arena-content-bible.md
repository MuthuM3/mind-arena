# Arena Content Bible

## What Is an Arena?

An Arena is a structured, story-driven ~3-minute shared challenge designed to provoke deep human perspective-taking and individual reflection.

This document grounds content standards in [`docs/product/content-and-facilitation.md`](../product/content-and-facilitation.md).

## Authority and Derivation

Documents in `docs/experience/` organize implementation-facing experience specifications. They do not supersede existing approved authority documents.

When content conflicts, the repository authority order and the referenced approved source document prevail. Replacing or migrating an authoritative document requires an accepted EDR under `docs/decisions/` and updates to all readiness and execution references.

## What an Arena Must Create

An Arena must create genuine intellectual tension, surprising human perspectives, and permission for honest reflection without social or moral judgment.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Core Invariant:** The purpose of an Arena is not to force opinion change.
>
> Valid outcomes of an Arena experience include:
> - **Changed:** The participant shifted their position based on new reasoning.
> - **Clarified:** The participant maintained their stance but articulated their rationale more clearly.
> - **Reinforced:** The participant felt more confident in their position after stress-testing it against alternatives.
> - **More uncertain:** The participant recognized newfound complexity in the dilemma.
> - **Added conditions:** The participant identified specific context or caveats required for their choice.
> - **Discovered an assumption:** The participant uncovered an unexamined premise underlying their view.

## Situation Design

- Situations must present genuine dilemmas with competing valid human values or priorities.
- Must establish immutable facts, explicit constraints, and clear stakes.
- Must avoid obvious "correct" answers or moral trap choices.
- Core situation must be understandable within 30 seconds in moderated testing.

## Choice Design

- Choices must be structurally balanced with equal visual and textual weight.
- No choice option may carry implicit system approval, stigma, or moral superiority.

## Perspective Selection & Contrast

- Perspectives selected for reveal must represent distinct, well-reasoned human viewpoints.
- Selection prioritizes reasoning contrast (different assumptions, priorities, or values) rather than extreme polarities or emotional hostility.
- Avoid false balance: do not give unsupported or unsafe claims equal standing for symmetry.

## Human Perspective Integrity

- Real human perspectives remain human-authored and attributed at the appropriate pseudonymous privacy level.
- AI never fabricates pseudo-human perspectives or modifies user text without explicit author approval.

## Build, Challenge, Question Prompts

- **Build:** Prompts invite the participant to strengthen, extend, or add supporting context to an encountered perspective.
- **Challenge:** Prompts invite the participant to identify potential risks, edge cases, or unexamined assumptions in an encountered perspective.
- **Question:** Prompts invite the participant to ask a clarifying question about the premises or boundaries of an encountered perspective.

## Reconsideration

Reconsideration presents the participant's original stance alongside encountered human reasoning and world consequences, granting equal dignity to Keeping, Refining, or Changing their position.

## Mind Shift

Mind Shift provides a private summary reflection mapping the lineage of the participant's thought process from initial commitment to final conclusion.

## AI Facilitation Boundaries

- AI framing must be neutral, curious, and clear.
- AI must never judge, grade, praise, scold, or score a participant's perspective or decision.
- AI never assigns moral, intellectual, or personality scores.

## Sensitive Topics & Safety Review

- V1 topics are curated and low-risk. High-risk political, hate-speech, self-harm, or explicitly abusive topics are strictly excluded.
- Every scenario package and perspective pool must pass safety review before launch.

## Copy and Time Budgets

The following authoring budgets govern content creation across the Arena journey:

| Element | Initial Budget |
| :--- | :--- |
| Title | 2–6 words |
| Invitation stakes line | at most 24 words |
| Core situation | at most 90 words, staged if needed |
| Role summary | at most 30 words per role |
| Optional definition | at most 24 words |
| Participant reason | target 10–50 words |
| Perspective card | target 20–70 words |
| Structured response | target 10–80 words |
| Consequence | at most 70 words |
| Mind Shift field | one short sentence unless expanded |

## What an Arena Must Never Become

- A debate stage or comment fight section.
- A survey or opinion poll.
- A moral test or personality quiz.
- A game with winners, losers, scores, or leaderboards.

## Arena Review Checklist

- [ ] Clear dilemma with competing valid values.
- [ ] Immutable facts and explicit constraints defined.
- [ ] Balanced choices with equal weight.
- [ ] Authentic human perspective pool available.
- [ ] All 6 valid reflection outcomes treated with equal dignity.
- [ ] Reading budget compliant (situation understandable within 30 seconds).
- [ ] Safety and age-eligibility boundaries verified.
