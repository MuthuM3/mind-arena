# Experience Manifesto

Mind Arena exists to improve human-to-human thinking through structured, shared reflection.

## Authority and Derivation

Documents in `docs/experience/` organize implementation-facing experience specifications. They do not supersede existing approved authority documents.

When content conflicts, the repository authority order and the referenced approved source document prevail. Replacing or migrating an authoritative document requires an accepted EDR under `docs/decisions/` and updates to all readiness and execution references.

## Core Experience Principles

- **Curiosity over Certainty:** Mind Arena invites participants into a calm space where exploring multiple viewpoints is inherently rewarding.
- **Discovery:** Every Arena offers surprising, authentic human perspectives—moments that provoke "I never thought about it that way."
- **Reflection:** The core interaction is quiet, unhurried thought. Users commit privately before encountering others to protect independent reasoning.
- **Contribution:** Real human reasoning powers the experience. Participants contribute their perspective to enrich collective insight.
- **Mind Shift:** Movement in thought—whether changing, clarifying, reinforcing, or identifying new conditions—is recognized equally.
- **Reflective Delight:** The experience feels surprisingly satisfying, calm, responsive, and clear without relying on addictive gamification or social validation loops.
- **AI as Facilitator:** AI structures the scenario, maintains pace, and reflects lineage. AI never judges, scores, or acts as the authority on truth.
- **Humans as the Source of Perspectives:** Real people remain the sole source of social perspective. AI never fabricates pseudo-human opinions.
- **No Winner Mentality:** There are no debates to win, points to score, or leaderboards to climb.
- **No Judgmental Scoring:** Mind Arena never assigns moral, intellectual, or personality scores.
- **Memorable over Merely Functional:** Mind Arena prioritizes temporal harmony, atmosphere, and meaningful friction over mechanical utility.

## What Should Mind Arena Feel Like?

In three minutes, Mind Arena should feel like stepping into a focused, low-pressure thinking space where a complex situation becomes clearer and another person's genuine reasoning becomes tangible.
