# Business Model Direction

## Principle

People pay for access to consistently valuable thinking experiences and better collaboration—not
for named AI models or unlimited generated text.

V1 has no payment. First prove meaningful exchange, repeat behavior, trust, and safe perspective
supply.

## Value ladder

### Daily Arena

The accessible consumer habit:

- one curated Today’s Arena;
- private Mind Shift history;
- spoiler-safe result cards and Friend Arena invitations;
- contribution and response notifications;
- basic thinking-pattern reflection;
- strong privacy and safety controls.

This should retain a meaningful free experience because the network needs diverse perspectives.

### Live Arena

Potential individual subscription:

- more live small-group sessions;
- topic packs;
- invite-based rooms;
- richer facilitated reflections;
- voice input after safety validation;
- user-controlled growth history.

### Purpose Arena

Potential group and organization plans:

- private brainstorming and decision rooms;
- repeatable facilitation templates;
- shared synthesis;
- organization-controlled retention;
- host and moderation tools;
- integrations and administration.

Workplace plans must never use individual “thinking scores” for performance evaluation.

## Marketplace possibility

Trusted educators, communities, or facilitators may eventually publish arena formats. This is
deferred until content quality, moderation, discovery, rights, and revenue-sharing systems are
mature.

## Unit economics

Measure:

- AI and moderation cost per Meaningful Perspective Exchange;
- human review cost per eligible contribution;
- content/editorial cost per active arena;
- notification and infrastructure cost per retained participant;
- acquisition cost by cohort;
- paid conversion only after repeated value.

Use deterministic systems for state, matching constraints, moderation rules, and summaries when AI
adds no measurable value. AI calls are task-specific and bounded.

## Network economics

Free participants are not merely leads; their high-quality perspectives create value for others.
Pricing cannot remove too much perspective diversity from the common pool.

Potential principles:

- core Daily Arena remains broadly accessible;
- paid value comes from frequency, depth, privacy boundaries, hosting, and workflow;
- contributors keep control over where their perspective can be used;
- no sale of reasoning data;
- no advertising based on inferred beliefs;
- no pay-to-amplify a perspective.

## Monetization gate

Test payment only after:

1. the atomic exchange passes usefulness and safety gates;
2. cohorts show voluntary repeat participation;
3. perspective supply remains healthy without founder seeding;
4. users name a recurring outcome they want more often;
5. the paid capability has measured operating cost;
6. monetization does not corrupt contribution quality or matching fairness.
